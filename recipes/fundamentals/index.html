    Company Fundamentals¶

    A bond has no EBITDA. Often its issuer has none either. Virgin Media O2 issues bonds through eight vehicles, and the accounts a credit analyst reads belong to none of them. So before you ask for leverage, you have to find the company that reports it. BQL's fundamentalTicker() does that for bonds and loans.

    Changed in May 2026

    In May 2026 Bloomberg changed how it chooses that company. It now skips financing vehicles and SPVs, and it prefers operating or holding companies with current, frequently reported accounts.

    Data on this page

    This page starts from bonds, as a credit investor does. It covers European high-yield issuers (Virgin Media O2, INEOS, Altice, Telecom Italia, iliad), the AT1 issuers SEB, Handelsbanken and Swedbank, and the Bloomberg Pan-European High Yield index.

    Amounts are in millions. The outputs below are live data as of 2026-11-17 and will differ when you run the queries. Before you adapt a query, read the Pitfalls.

    Quick reference¶

    You want Use One row per
    The company whose accounts cover a bond or loan fundamentalTicker(<bonds>) as the universe company
    A company's figure next to each bond value(<field>, fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE) bond
    Annual, half-year, quarterly or LTM figures with(fa_period_type=A, fa_period_offset=range(-1Y, 0Y)), fpt=S, Q, LTM company and period
    Reported or adjusted figures fa_adjusted=N or Y; fa_act_est_data=A for actuals company and period
    Revenue per business line or country segments('<equity>', type=reported, hierarchy=PRODUCT) or hierarchy=LOCATION segment and period
    Bank capital and profitability bs_tier1_com_equity_ratio(), normalized_roe() with fpt=Q bank and quarter
    A statistic per sector median(group(<field>, classification_name(BCLASS, 3))) over the index's companies sector
    Bloomberg Intelligence's sector figures bq.bsrch("BI:TPD", overrides={"BIKEY": ...}) row of the template

    The period parameters work on any fundamental field, either in with(...) for the whole query or on one field, as in ebitda(fpt=LTM).

    Parameter Short Example values Meaning
    fa_period_type fpt A, S, Q, LTM annual, half-year, quarter, last twelve months
    fa_period_offset fpo 0, -1, range(-3Q, 0Q) periods counted back from the company's latest report
    fa_period_reference fpr 2026Q3, range(2026Q1, 2026Q3) calendar periods, the same for every company
    fa_adjusted N, Y as reported, or Bloomberg-adjusted
    fa_act_est_data A, E actuals or estimates
    currency EUR the currency of the result; check the CURRENCY column

    From a bond to the company that reports¶

    value(..., fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE)

    For each ticker, the issuing entities of its live bonds, the company whose accounts fundamentalTicker uses, and the capital structure (CAST) parent.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

query = """
let(#accounts = value(name, fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE);
    #cast = value(name, parent(type=CAST), mapby=LINEAGE);)
get(ticker, issuer(), #accounts, #cast)
for(filter(bondsUniv(ACTIVE, consolidateduplicates=True),
           ticker in ['VMED', 'INEGRP', 'ALTICE', 'TITIM', 'ILDFP', 'HEIBOS', 'SEB']))
"""
with BQuery() as bq:
    df = bq.bql(query).combine(on="ID")
print(
    df.group_by("ticker", "#accounts", "#cast")
    .agg(issuers=pl.col("issuer()").n_unique(), bonds=pl.len())
    .sort("ticker", "bonds", descending=[False, True])
)

    Watch out

    • Heimstaden Bostad bonds map to Heimstaden AB, although Bostad publishes its own accounts. For a subsidiary's own figures, query its equity directly: issuerOf('<bond>') gives 1546973D SS Equity for Heimstaden Bostad.
    • The projected items add SOURCE_ID, so join on ID.

    Notes

    • Eight Virgin Media O2 vehicles, from Virgin Media Secured Finance to the vendor financing DACs, lead to one set of accounts. Bloomberg's own example is INEOS: the old logic mapped its bonds to the issuer INEOS Finance plc, the new one maps them to INEOS Group Holdings SA.
    • fundamentalTicker and the CAST parent answer different questions. The CAST parent of Telecom Italia is Poste Italiane, its largest shareholder. The accounts stay with Telecom Italia, which reports them. The CAST parent is the top of the capital structure; fundamentalTicker is the company whose figures matter for the bond.
    • One TITIM bond is issued by TIM SA, the Brazilian subsidiary, and maps to TIM's own accounts.

    Annual revenue and EBITDA, last two years¶

    fundamentalticker(...), fa_period_type=A, fa_period_offset=range(-1Y, 0Y)

    Revenue, EBITDA and EBITDA margin of the high-yield companies above, for the last two fiscal years, in EUR millions.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

query = """
get(name, sales_rev_turn / 1M as #revenue, ebitda / 1M as #ebitda)
for(fundamentalticker(filter(bondsUniv(ACTIVE, consolidateduplicates=True),
                             ticker in ['VMED', 'INEGRP', 'ALTICE', 'TITIM', 'ILDFP'])))
with(fa_period_type=A, fa_period_offset=range(-1Y, 0Y), currency=EUR)
preferences(dropCols=['AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE'])
"""
with BQuery() as bq:
    df = bq.bql(query).combine()
table = df.sort("name", "PERIOD_END_DATE").select(
    "name",
    "PERIOD_END_DATE",
    revenue=pl.col("#revenue").round().cast(pl.Int64),
    ebitda=pl.col("#ebitda").round().cast(pl.Int64),
    margin_pct=(100 * pl.col("#ebitda") / pl.col("#revenue")).round(1),
    ebitda_chg_pct=(100 * pl.col("#ebitda").pct_change().over("name")).round(1),
)
with pl.Config(tbl_rows=20):
    print(table)

    Watch out

    • fa_period_offset=range(-1Y, 0Y) is the last two years each company has reported. A company with a March year-end gets other dates than one with a December year-end, so keep PERIOD_END_DATE.
    • dropCols=['AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE']: two fields of the same year can carry different revision dates, and combine() would then put them on two rows.

    Notes

    • fundamentalticker(...) around a bond screen returns each company once, however many bonds and issuers it has.

    Reported vs adjusted EBITDA¶

    fa_adjusted=N / Y, fa_act_est_data=A

    Bloomberg's adjusted EBITDA next to the reported one, for the last fiscal year.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

query = """
get(name,
    ebitda(fa_adjusted=N) / 1M as #reported,
    ebitda(fa_adjusted=Y) / 1M as #adjusted)
for(fundamentalticker(filter(bondsUniv(ACTIVE, consolidateduplicates=True),
                             ticker in ['VMED', 'INEGRP', 'ALTICE', 'TITIM', 'ILDFP'])))
with(fa_period_type=A, fa_act_est_data=A, currency=EUR)
preferences(dropCols=['AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE'])
"""
with BQuery() as bq:
    df = bq.bql(query).combine()
print(
    df.sort("name").select(
        "name",
        "PERIOD_END_DATE",
        reported=pl.col("#reported").round().cast(pl.Int64),
        adjusted=pl.col("#adjusted").round().cast(pl.Int64),
        adj_pct=(100 * (pl.col("#adjusted") / pl.col("#reported") - 1)).round(1),
    )
)

    Revenue by product and by country¶

    segments(..., hierarchy=PRODUCT), hierarchy=LOCATION

    iliad, the French telecom group, reports revenue per business line and per country. One request per hierarchy, then one table.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

template = """
let(#segment = segment_name();
    #revenue = sales_rev_turn(fpt=S, fpo=range(-1S, 0S)) / 1M;)
get(#segment, #revenue)
for(segments('ILD FP Equity', type=reported, hierarchy={hierarchy}, level=1))
"""
frames = []
with BQuery() as bq:
    for hierarchy in ["PRODUCT", "LOCATION"]:
        df = bq.bql(template.format(hierarchy=hierarchy)).combine()
        frames.append(df.with_columns(hierarchy=pl.lit(hierarchy)))
print(
    pl.concat(frames, how="diagonal_relaxed")
    .with_columns(half=pl.col("PERIOD_END_DATE").cast(pl.String))
    .pivot(on="half", index=["hierarchy", "#segment"], values="#revenue")
)

    • Banks report segments too, but sales_rev_turn per segment is null for SEB.

    Notes

    • iliad reports half-years, hence fpt=S. For a quarterly reporter use fpt=Q and fpo=range(-1Q, 0Q).
    • segments() needs the equity ticker. Get it from a bond with fundamentalticker (first recipe): the ILDFP bonds map to ILD FP Equity.
    • Segment rows that do not add up, such as Adjustment or Intra-group sales, are part of the reported split. A segment without a figure for a period is null.

    Swedish AT1 issuers: CET1 and ROE by quarter¶

    bs_tier1_com_equity_ratio(), normalized_roe(), fpt=Q

    Start from the AT1 bonds of SEB, Handelsbanken and Swedbank. fundamentalTicker turns them into the three banks; then fetch four quarters of CET1 ratio and return on equity, in percent.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

query = """
get(bs_tier1_com_equity_ratio() as #cet1, normalized_roe() as #roe)
for(fundamentalticker(filter(bondsUniv(ACTIVE, consolidateduplicates=True),
    ticker in ['SEB', 'SHBASS', 'SWEDA'] and
    basel_iii_designation() == 'Additional Tier 1')))
with(fpt=Q, fpo=range(-3Q, 0Q))
preferences(dropCols=['AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE'])
"""
with BQuery() as bq:
    df = bq.bql(query).combine()
print(
    df.select(
        "PERIOD_END_DATE",
        bank=pl.col("ID").str.split(" ").list.first(),
        cet1=pl.col("#cet1"),
        roe=pl.col("#roe").round(1),
    )
    .sort("bank")
    .pivot(on="bank", index="PERIOD_END_DATE", values=["cet1", "roe"])
    .sort("PERIOD_END_DATE")
)

    Output

    fpt=Q, fpo=range(-3Q, 0Q) is the last four reported quarters. Before you compare banks, check that PERIOD_END_DATE is the same for all of them.

    • The bank tickers (SEBA, SHBA, SWEDA) come from the mapping, not from the query. The same code works for any list of bank bonds.
    • For CET1 against the AT1 trigger of each bond, see AT1 distance to trigger.

    How much of a high-yield index has fundamentals?¶

    issuerOf vs parent(type=CAST) vs fundamentalticker

    Three ways to reach a company from each bond of the Pan-European High Yield index (LP01TREU): the issuer, the CAST parent and fundamentalTicker. For each, the bonds and index weight with a last-twelve-months net debt / EBITDA, and the number of companies they lead to.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

query = """
get(id().weights as #weight,
    value(net_debt_to_ebitda(fpt=LTM), issuerOf, mapby=LINEAGE) as #issuerOf,
    value(net_debt_to_ebitda(fpt=LTM), parent(type=CAST), mapby=LINEAGE) as #cast,
    value(net_debt_to_ebitda(fpt=LTM), fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE) as #fundamentalTicker)
for(members('LP01TREU Index'))
"""
with BQuery() as bq:
    result = bq.bql(query)
labels = {
    "#issuerOf": "issuerOf",
    "#cast": "parent(type=CAST)",
    "#fundamentalTicker": "fundamentalTicker",
}
weights = result[0].select("ID", "#weight")
rows = []
for name, item in zip(result.names[1:], result.dataframes[1:]):
    covered = item.filter(pl.col(name).is_not_null()).join(weights, on="ID")
    rows.append(
        {
            "mapping": labels[name],
            "bonds": covered.height,
            "pct_bonds": round(100 * covered.height / weights.height, 1),
            "pct_weight": round(covered["#weight"].sum(), 1),
            "companies": covered["SOURCE_ID"].n_unique(),
        }
    )
print(pl.DataFrame(rows))

    Output 

    shape: (3, 5)
┌───────────────────┬───────┬───────────┬────────────┬───────────┐
│ mapping           ┆ bonds ┆ pct_bonds ┆ pct_weight ┆ companies │
│ ---               ┆ ---   ┆ ---       ┆ ---        ┆ ---       │
│ str               ┆ i64   ┆ f64       ┆ f64        ┆ i64       │
╞═══════════════════╪═══════╪═══════════╪════════════╪═══════════╡
│ issuerOf          ┆ 417   ┆ 55.6      ┆ 56.7       ┆ 194       │
│ parent(type=CAST) ┆ 430   ┆ 57.3      ┆ 57.6       ┆ 188       │

└───────────────────┴───────┴───────────┴────────────┴───────────┘

    Watch out

    The CAST parent can be a shareholder, such as Poste Italiane for Telecom Italia, and a shareholder's leverage is not the bond's.

    Notes

    • fundamentalTicker finds a leverage figure for about three bonds in four, the other two mappings for a little over half. It skips financing vehicles without accounts, and it reaches companies that issue through a vehicle.
    • companies counts the companies with a figure. Over the whole index, fundamentalTicker leads to fewer companies than issuerOf, because it puts the vehicles of one group under one company. More of those companies have figures, though.
    • id().weights is the index weight in percent; the weights add up to 100.
    • The three items are used one by one here, because each has its own SOURCE_ID and PERIOD_END_DATE.

    Median leverage per sector¶

    median(group(..., classification_name(BCLASS, 3)))

    Median net debt / EBITDA per Bloomberg (BCLASS) sector, over the companies behind the Pan-European High Yield index. The query runs twice, once over the index's issuers (issuerOf) and once over fundamentalTicker.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

template = """
let(#sector = classification_name(BCLASS, 3);
    #lev = group(net_debt_to_ebitda(fpt=LTM), #sector);)
get(count(#lev) as #n, median(#lev) as #median)
for({universe}(members('LP01TREU Index')))
preferences(dropCols=['ORIG_IDS', 'AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE', 'PERIOD_END_DATE'])
"""
tables = []
with BQuery() as bq:
    for universe in ["issuerOf", "fundamentalTicker"]:
        df = bq.bql(template.format(universe=universe)).combine(on="ID")
        tables.append(
            df.select(
                sector=pl.col("ID").replace({"NullGroup": "(no BCLASS sector)"}),
                n=pl.col("#n"),
                median=pl.col("#median").round(1),
            )
        )
table = (
    tables[0]
    .join(tables[1], on="sector", how="full", coalesce=True, suffix="_ft")
    .sort("n_ft", descending=True, nulls_last=True)
)
with pl.Config(tbl_rows=30):
    print(table)

    Watch out

    • Drop PERIOD_END_DATE and REVISION_DATE from grouped items. A group's dates come from one of its companies and differ between count and median, so the two items would not join.
    • Join on ID, the group's name. The group of companies without a sector has ID NullGroup and a null #SECTOR, and combine() does not match null keys.
    • Leverage of banks and other financials means something else; read those rows with care.

    Notes

    • n is the number of companies with a figure, median the median net debt / EBITDA. Columns ending in _ft are for fundamentalTicker.
    • fundamentalTicker gives more companies with a figure in most sectors. Some of the companies it chooses have no BCLASS sector of their own; they end up in (no BCLASS sector).
    • For an index-weighted figure, Bloomberg's announcement averages over the bonds: wavg(group(#x, #sector), group(weights, #sector)) with #x = value(<field>, fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE) over members(...). An average of ratios moves a lot with a few companies whose EBITDA is near zero or negative, so check the inputs.

    Bloomberg Intelligence sector template¶

    BSRCH: bsrch("BI:TPD", {"BIKEY": ...})

    Bloomberg Intelligence (BI) publishes sector figures as dashboards. Each one has a BIKEY, the key in the =@BSRCH("BI:TPD","BIKEY=...") formula it exports to Excel. bsrch() gets the same grid. This one has net leverage and spread per turn of leverage by rating.

    import polars as pl
from polars_bloomberg import BQuery

with BQuery() as bq:
    grid = bq.bsrch(
        "BI:TPD", overrides={"BIKEY": "DKOCVGXJVU8II8M90W8JSQEKR", "LIMIT": 20000}
    )
quarters = [c for c in grid.columns if c[:2].isdigit()]
tidy = grid.unpivot(
    index=["Main_Category_Custom_Name", "Category"], on=quarters, variable_name="quarter"
).with_columns(pl.col("quarter").str.to_date("%m/%d/%Y"))
latest = tidy["quarter"].max()
print(f"{len(quarters)} quarters, the latest {latest}")
print(
    tidy.filter(
        pl.col("quarter") == latest,
        pl.col("Main_Category_Custom_Name").str.contains("by Rating|Spread Per Turn"),
        pl.col("Category").is_in(["BB", "B", "CCC"]),
    )
    .with_columns(
        rating=pl.col("Category").cast(pl.Enum(["BB", "B", "CCC"])),
        metric=pl.when(pl.col("Main_Category_Custom_Name").str.starts_with("Spread"))
        .then(pl.lit("spread_per_turn_bp"))
        .otherwise(pl.lit("net_leverage")),
        value=pl.col("value").round(1),
    )
    .pivot(on="metric", index="rating", values="value")
    .sort("rating")
)

    Notes

    • The grid has a column per quarter with the date as MM/DD/YYYY text; unpivot and str.to_date make it a tidy table.
    • LIMIT set high avoids a "reached internal limit" warning; the grid is the same.

    Pitfalls¶

    What surprised us while writing these recipes.

    Symptom Cause Fix
    Leverage of a bond's issuer is missing The issuer is a financing vehicle without accounts fundamentalticker(...) as universe, or value(<field>, fundamentalticker, mapby=LINEAGE)
    Companies show different PERIOD_END_DATEs for the "same" quarter fpo=0 is each company's latest report, not a calendar quarter Compare PERIOD_END_DATE; or use fpr=2026Q3 for one calendar quarter (null where not yet reported)
    fill=PREV does not fill the missing quarter It does not carry a value into a period the company has not reported Use the latest reported period (fpo=0) and show its date
    fpt=LTM is null, but the company reports quarters (VMED O2 UK Holdings) Bloomberg has no LTM figure for that company Add up the last four quarters: sum(ebitda(fpt=Q, fpo=range(-3Q, 0Q)))
    A group with a null key is split over two rows combine() does not match null keys such as the null #SECTOR of NullGroup combine(on="ID"); the group's ID is never null
    A company appears on two rows after combine() Fields carry different REVISION_DATEs, or grouped items different PERIOD_END_DATEs preferences(dropCols=['AS_OF_DATE', 'REVISION_DATE']), plus 'PERIOD_END_DATE' for groups
    count(group(id)) over fundamentalticker(members(...)) returns the number of bonds It counts the bonds that went in (750 for LP01TREU), not the companies Count the rows of get(name) for(fundamentalticker(...))
    segments(..., hierarchy=GEOGRAPHY) fails The hierarchy is called LOCATION hierarchy=LOCATION
    Segment revenue of a bank is null sales_rev_turn is not filled for bank segments Use group figures, or a field the bank reports per segment
    Fundamentals in the reporting currency although you asked for EUR with(ccy=EUR) is ignored without an error with(currency=EUR) or field(currency=EUR); check CURRENCY
